Because NVIDIA has been a pioneer in changing the architecture of its GPUs to perform general-purpose tasks, we present all GPU architectures developed by NVIDIA Corporation over the last two decades in this paper. We will also look at the major hardware differences along with all the software platforms and solutions that have been introduced in these architectures. This survey should be of particular interest to researchers who are interested in using NVIDIA’s GPUs for developing their high-performance applications.
