Given NVIDIA’s leading role in evolving GPUs toward general-purpose computing, this paper provides an overview of GPU architectures developed by the company over the past two decades. It examines key hardware advancements alongside the corresponding software platforms and frameworks introduced across these generations. The survey is particularly relevant for researchers aiming to leverage NVIDIA GPUs for high-performance applications
