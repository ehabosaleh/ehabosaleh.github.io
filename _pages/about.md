I am an HPC researcher specializing in systems programming and high-performance communication. My work focuses on designing efficient communication frameworks for distributed and parallel systems, with particular emphasis on MPI, OpenMP, and shared memory architectures.

I develop low-level, performance-critical software in C/C++, exploring scalable communication models, one-sided communication, and proxy-based execution strategies. My research aims to improve data movement efficiency and overall system performance in large-scale computing environments.

I am currently a Research Associate at the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre (LRZ), where I work on advanced HPC communication and system-level optimization.
